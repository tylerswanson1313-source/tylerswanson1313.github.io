Mission Statement

Driven by the urgent need for resilient climate solutions, my work focuses on the intersection of community sustainability, agricultural systems, and environmental policy. By evaluating emerging frameworks like agrivoltaics, I strive to generate evidence-based research that empowers local communities, balances land-use priorities, and fosters equitable renewable energy transitions.

Key Values

Community-Centered Research

Prioritizing local voices, landscape ideologies, and stakeholder experiences to guide top-down policy design.

Interdisciplinary Collaboration

Bridging environmental economics, human geography, and agricultural law to tackle complex climate challenges.

Environmental Stewardship

Advancing dual-use solar and land strategies that protect rural ecosystems while scaling renewable energy capacities.

Actionable Policy Analysis

Translating academic data into practical legal frameworks, fact sheets, and tools for real-world governance.
